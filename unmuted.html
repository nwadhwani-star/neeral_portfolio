Individual class project · Visual identity · Event website · Framer

UNMUTED

I developed UNMUTED as a class event concept exploring anxiety through art. The visual identity and website introduce the idea, its activities, and support resources.

A scroll-through of my finished website · Muted preview

The starting point

I wanted the event to feel welcoming to young adults who may find public sharing uncomfortable. The concept makes space for expression without pressure to speak.

Building a visual language

I developed a brand kit with logo variations, typography, colors, textures, and imagery. I used Framer to assemble the event website and organize its schedule and supporting information.

Project scope

This is an individual student concept, not a documented live event. The site was assembled in Framer; its existing page title identifies a Veltrix template. I present the branding and event content as my project work.
