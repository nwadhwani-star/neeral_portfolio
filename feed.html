Neeral’s Feed

I created Neeral’s Feed as a personal website that brings together moments from my life. A soft pink visual identity introduces a feed of everyday experiences.

A scroll-through of my finished website · Muted preview

The starting point

I wanted to translate my own interests and everyday moments into a website with a recognizable personality. The landing page sets the tone before visitors enter the feed.

A personal visual direction

The site uses a pink palette, expressive typography, and a simple entrance into the content. My focus was on giving the project a consistent look and making it feel like an extension of me.

Project scope

This is an individual class website. It demonstrates visual styling and web presentation; I have not measured its usability through a formal study.
